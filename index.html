Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers regarding auditor training, certificates, online modes, and corporate delivery.

ISO auditor training is a structured professional educational program that teaches participants how to evaluate, audit, and improve organizational management systems in compliance with specific International Organization for Standardization (ISO) requirements. It covers ISO 19011 auditing principles, audit planning, gathering objective evidence, non-conformity identification, and reporting.
An Internal Auditor course (typically 16 hours) prepares professionals to conduct first-party (internal) audits within their own organization. A Lead Auditor course (typically 40 hours) is an advanced, comprehensive qualification that prepares senior auditors to lead second-party (supplier) and third-party (external certification body) audit teams, manage full audit lifecycles, and render certification decisions.
